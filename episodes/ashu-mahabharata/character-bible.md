# Character bible — Ashu Mahabharata

This file will grow as each reel is built. The shared implementation lives in `video/src/Ashu/mahabharata/kit/cast.tsx`.

## Reel 1 cast
- Barbarik: warm brown skin gradient, orange warrior cloth, teal sash, black hair/topknot, three glowing arrows, composed and devotional.
- Krishna: blue skin gradient, gold robe, teal sash, peacock feather crown, gold vertical tilak; disguised brahmin uses cream robes and muted ornament.
- Bhishma: all white, white beard/pagri.
- Drona: cream/ochre, white beard, red/tilak detail.
- Barbarik's mother: warm skin, maroon/gold sari, blessing hand.
- Bhima/Ghatotkacha: giant dark sky silhouettes.
- Shiva: luminous abstract silhouette, crescent, trishul.
- Khatu Shyam: serene golden-lit head, eyes open/peaceful, divine but not gory.

## Reusable design rules
- Faces: almond eyes with glint, strong brows, nose bridge/nostril, lip shadow, cheek shading, warm/cool rim depending on scene.
- Costumes: at least one gradient, one shadow fold, one trim/ornament layer.
- Characters must read at phone size first; detail should enrich close-ups without creating noise.

## Reel 2 additions
- Karna: warm bronze skin, black warrior topknot, golden chest-fused kavach with sun disk and large gold kundal, cream dhoti; without armour the same face and costume but bare-chested under a simple cloth wrap, still dignified.
- Indra: crowned golden-brown deva, white-gold garments, firm eyes; in clouds he is large, luminous and dignified, never horror-like.
- Surya: a huge warm sun-face/mandala in the sky, symbolic and serene rather than a literal human close-up.
