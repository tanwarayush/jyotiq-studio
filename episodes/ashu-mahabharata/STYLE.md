# Ashu Mahabharata series style

## Canvas and finish
- 1080×1920, 30 fps, vertical.
- Painted 2D animation: simple readable silhouettes, but with modelled faces, cloth gradients, rim light, haze, grain and vignette.
- Four depth layers minimum: sky/far background, midground set, characters, foreground smoke/grass/embers, then grain/vignette/light.
- No Jyotiq branding, no app panel, no logo bug. End card only: series title, next teaser, `Follow @ashuanimates`.

## Typography
- Captions: Mukta ExtraBold/Bold, white, 58–66 px for short phrases, 3–4 px dark shadow, key spoken word highlighted in amber/orange.
- Hook/title/end card: Rozha One, warm cream/gold, 78–110 px.
- Captions are progressive and exact to the approved words. Only 2–5 visible words per chunk wherever the line allows.
- Text never enters top 8%, right 15%, or bottom 20%. Caption target: y 1370–1595.

## Character bible: initial series rules
- Krishna: blue skin with cool shadow and cyan rim, gold dhoti/robe, peacock feather crown, vertical gold tilak, calm half-smile. As brahmin: same actor, muted cream/ochre robe, feather/crown reduced or hidden.
- Barbarik: warm brown skin, orange/ochre warrior cloth, teal sash, black topknot, quiver of exactly three arrows, young but disciplined face, never comic or afraid.
- Bhishma: all white/cream, white beard and pagri, old but upright.
- Drona: white/ochre brahmin-warrior, white beard, tripundra/red tilak.
- Ghatotkacha/Bhima: giant distant silhouettes only in reel 1; no detailed faces needed.
- Shiva: symbolic blue-white glow, crescent, trident and seated silhouette; dignified and abstract.
- Khatu Shyam: serene golden head/icon, peaceful eyes, no gore.
- No animals in close-up unless fully designed; avoid the old “horse reads as goat” failure.

## Motion grammar
- Every shot uses a slow push/pull/pan/tilt/drift. No static shots.
- Camera moves ease in/out; use small deterministic handheld noise.
- Big shake/flash/deep hit only 2–4 times per reel.
- Character idle: breathing, blinking, hair/cloth sway, jewellery/arrow glow.
- Cuts land on line starts or key words. Most shots 2–4 s; emotional/devotional shots may hold 4–6 s.
